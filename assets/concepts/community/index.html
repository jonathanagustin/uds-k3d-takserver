01 / Recommended: connected stack

A literal alternative for the integrated platform identity. Three substantial layers connect to a navigation heading. At 16px each layer and the connecting bus are 2px thick. The 2px gaps preserve the three layers. The navy heading outline preserves the silhouette on white.

02 / Alternative: waypoint relay

A teammate node links through a U-shaped route to a heading. The route reads clearly at 16px, but says less about an integrated platform. The stack is the stronger combined TAK and UDS identity.
